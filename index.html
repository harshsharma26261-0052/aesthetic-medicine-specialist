FAQ

Most treatments are very well tolerated. We use topical numbing cream before injections and very fine needles. Most patients describe it as a minor pinch.
Botox typically lasts 3–4 months. Dermal fillers last 9–18 months depending on the product and area. Skin boosters last around 6 months. We will advise on your personal maintenance plan.
Most treatments have minimal to no downtime. You may experience mild swelling or redness for 24–48 hours. Bruising is possible but uncommon. Most clients return to normal activities the same day.
Prices vary by treatment and amount of product used. We provide a full quote after your consultation. There is never any pressure to proceed on the day.
